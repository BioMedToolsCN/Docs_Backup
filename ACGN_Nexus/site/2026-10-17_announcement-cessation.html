Cessation Notice / 停刊公告

To the readers, authors, and academic community of ACGN Nexus:

After careful consideration, ACGN Nexus will officially cease publication as of October 2026. Our latest issue was published in October 2026, and we were honored to serve the interdisciplinary field of ACGN studies.

All published articles will remain open access, and their DOIs will remain permanently valid. We commit to:
- All published article DOIs will continue to be accessible
- Website archives will be maintained until at least May 2027

We thank all authors, reviewers, and readers who have supported this journal.

ACGN Nexus Editorial Board
