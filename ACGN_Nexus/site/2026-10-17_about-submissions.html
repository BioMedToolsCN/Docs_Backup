Author Guidelines

ACGN Nexus welcomes submissions across four sections. Please identify which section best fits your work:

Nexus Papers – Original research connecting ACGN culture with social science
Ethics Arena – Ethical inquiry and debate on ACGN-related moral issues
Deep Dive – In-depth critical commentary and analysis
Crossover – Interdisciplinary dialogues, interviews, and collaborative pieces
Community – A forum for academic correspondence, editorial announcements, and lighter content celebrating ACGN culture. Includes three sub-types: Announcements (editorial notices), Correspondence (short scholarly responses to published work), and Omake (informal, humorous, or creative pieces).


MANUSCRIPT FORMAT

File format: Word (.docx)
Languages accepted: Chinese or English
Font: 12pt, 1.5 or double-spaced
Word limits: See individual Section Policies


MANUSCRIPT STRUCTURE

For Nexus Papers and Ethics Arena:
Authors may choose any coherent structure suited to their argument. Common approaches include:
- Thematic structure (organized by themes or concepts)
- Argumentative structure (thesis → evidence → counterargument → conclusion)
- Narrative structure (chronological or case-based progression)
- Comparative structure (analyzing parallels across works, cultures, or theories)
The traditional IMRaD format (Introduction-Methods-Results-Discussion) is not required but is acceptable if appropriate.

For Deep Dive:
Free-form critical essay. No prescribed structure, but should have a clear analytical thread.

For Crossover:
Format depends on the piece type (dialogue, interview, joint essay). Contact the editorial office to discuss format before submission.


WRITING STYLE

First-person narration and subjective argumentation are permitted across all sections. Authors are encouraged to develop a distinctive voice while maintaining intellectual rigor.

However, all claims—whether empirical or interpretive—should be supported by evidence, citations, or well-reasoned argument. Unsupported assertions and logical fallacies will be flagged during editorial review.


LANGUAGE POLICY

Chinese and English submissions are both welcome.
- Chinese manuscripts must include an English abstract (200–300 words) and 3–6 English keywords.
- English manuscripts must include a Chinese abstract (300–500 characters) and 3–6 Chinese keywords. If unable to provide Chinese translations, the editorial team can assist.


REFERENCES

Use a consistent citation format throughout the manuscript. We recommend but do not mandate APA 7th edition. Other widely recognized formats (Chicago, MLA, GB/T 7714) are acceptable, provided they are applied consistently.

For ACGN-specific sources (anime episodes, game versions, manga chapters, visual novel routes, etc.), please provide sufficient identifying information for readers to locate the referenced material. 


FIGURES AND IMAGES

When using copyrighted images from ACGN works (screenshots, artwork, etc.), authors must:
- Use images only to the extent necessary for critical analysis (fair use / fair dealing)
- Provide proper attribution (work title, creator, copyright holder, year)
- Not use images purely for decoration
- Indicate the source clearly in figure captions

The editorial team reserves the right to request removal or replacement of images that do not meet fair use standards.


DATA AVAILABILITY STATEMENT

All submissions must include a data availability statement. We recognize that ACGN research often relies on qualitative, interpretive, or media-based evidence rather than traditional datasets. Appropriate statements include:

- "All data supporting the findings of this study are available within the article and its supplementary materials."
- "This study is based on publicly available media works cited in the References section."
- "The datasets analyzed during this study are available from [repository name] at [URL/DOI]."
- "The interview transcripts/survey data that support this study are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request, subject to participant consent."

If your research involves quantitative data (surveys, content analysis coding sheets, network data, etc.), we encourage deposit in a public repository such as Zenodo, Figshare, or the Open Science Framework (OSF).


REFERENCE PRESERVATION POLICY

ACGN scholarship frequently cites web-based, ephemeral, or non-traditional sources—fan wikis, forum discussions, social media posts, streaming platform pages, official announcements, blog posts, and similar materials. These sources are vulnerable to deletion, modification, or link rot.

To ensure the long-term integrity of cited evidence, ACGN Nexus requires the following:

1. For all non-DOI, non-ISBN online sources: authors must archive the referenced page using the Wayback Machine (web.archive.org) or a similar public archiving service at the time of writing, and include the archived URL in the citation alongside the original URL.

2. If a public archive is not feasible (e.g., paywalled content, social media requiring login, dynamic web applications, game interfaces): authors must retain a local snapshot (screenshot, PDF printout, or screen recording) and be prepared to provide it to the editorial team upon request.

3. Citation format for archived sources:
   Username. (Year, Month Day). Title or description. Platform. Original URL (Archived at: archived URL, accessed YYYY-MM-DD)

4. For game content, visual novel routes, and other interactive media: provide sufficient identifying information (game title, version, platform, specific scene/chapter/route) for independent verification.

The editorial team may request snapshots or archived links during review. Failure to provide verifiable references may result in the removal of unsupported claims.


AI USAGE STATEMENT

Authors must disclose any use of generative AI tools (including but not limited to large language models, image generators, and AI-assisted translation or editing tools) in the preparation of their manuscript.

Required disclosures:
- Which AI tool(s) were used (name, version if known)
- What purpose(s) they served (e.g., drafting, editing, translation, literature search, data analysis, image generation, coding)
- Which sections or elements of the manuscript were produced or substantially assisted by AI

Disclosure should be placed in an "AI Usage Statement" section at the end of the manuscript, before the References.

Guidelines:
- AI tools may be used to assist with writing, translation, literature review, data analysis, and similar tasks.
- AI tools may NOT be listed as authors. Authorship requires intellectual accountability, which AI cannot bear.
- Authors remain fully responsible for the accuracy, originality, and integrity of all content, including any content generated or revised with AI assistance.
- AI-generated images used as figures must be clearly labeled as such.
- If no AI tools were used, authors may state: "No generative AI tools were used in the preparation of this manuscript."


CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

Authors must disclose any financial, personal, professional, or ideological conflicts of interest that could be perceived as influencing the work. This includes but is not limited to:
- Employment, consultancy, or financial relationships with companies, studios, or platforms discussed in the manuscript
- Personal relationships with creators or industry figures who are the subject of analysis
- Involvement in fan communities or organizations whose activities are analyzed in the manuscript

If there are no conflicts, authors should state: "The authors declare no conflicts of interest."


ARTICLE PROCESSING CHARGES

A flat article processing charge of 100 USD applies to all sections.

Promotional Policy: APC is currently waived for all submissions as a long-term introductory offer. Authors will not be charged during this promotional period. Any future changes to this policy will be announced in advance on the journal website.

Submission Preparation Checklist

By checking the items below, you confirm that your submission meets these requirements. Submissions that do not meet these requirements may be returned to the author.

- The submission has not been previously published, nor is it before another journal for consideration (or an explanation has been provided in Comments to the Editor).
- The submission file is in Word (.docx) format.
- The text meets the word count guidelines for the chosen section.
- The manuscript includes an abstract and keywords in both Chinese and English (or in the submission language with translation in the other).
- All references are properly cited using a consistent citation format.
- All online references without a DOI or ISBN have been archived (e.g., via the Wayback Machine), with archived URLs included in the citation. Where public archiving is not feasible, the author has retained local snapshots available upon editorial request.
- Any copyrighted images from ACGN works are used only for critical analysis purposes, with proper attribution provided.
- A data availability statement is included.
- If generative AI tools were used in preparing the manuscript, an AI Usage Statement has been included describing the tools and their purposes.
- All potential conflicts of interest have been disclosed (or a statement of no conflicts is included).
- All authors listed have approved the final manuscript and agree to its submission to ACGN Nexus.

Nexus Papers

Nexus Papers publishes original research articles that connect ACGN cultural phenomena with social science theories and frameworks. We welcome work drawing on sociology, psychology, philosophy, communication studies, law, economics, political science, aesthetics, and other disciplines.

Submissions are not required to follow the IMRaD structure. Authors may choose a manuscript structure that conforms to the academic conventions of their discipline or is otherwise best suited to the subject matter. First-person perspective and subjective argumentation are permitted, provided claims are well-supported and intellectually honest.

Manuscripts should demonstrate:
- A clear research question or analytical thesis
- Engagement with relevant scholarship (academic or critical)
- Proper citation of sources
- Logical coherence and depth of analysis

Word count: 5,000–12,000 words (Chinese) or 3,000–8,000 words (English)

Ethics Arena

Ethics Arena is dedicated to ethical inquiry within ACGN culture. We publish essays, debates, and analyses that examine the moral, philosophical, and societal implications of topics such as:

- AI-generated content and virtual character ethics
- Boundaries of derivative creation (fan works, doujinshi, modding)
- Representation of violence, gender, and identity in fictional media
- Privacy, consent, and parasocial relationships in fan communities
- The ethics of gatekeeping, censorship, and platform governance

Submissions may take the form of position papers, ethical case studies, dialogues, or critical essays. First-person argumentation and normative claims are welcome, but authors must engage with counterarguments and acknowledge the complexity of the issues discussed.

Both invited contributions and open submissions are accepted.

Word count: 3,000–8,000 words (Chinese) or 2,000–6,000 words (English)

Deep Dive

Deep Dive publishes in-depth critical commentary and analysis of specific ACGN works, events, trends, or controversies. Unlike Nexus Papers, Deep Dive pieces are more essayistic and opinion-driven, prioritizing depth of insight over formal methodology.

We welcome:
- Critical readings of individual works or franchises
- Analysis of industry events, controversies, or turning points
- Comparative studies across media, cultures, or time periods
- Long-form reviews that go beyond surface-level evaluation

First-person voice, subjective judgment, and passionate argumentation are all appropriate here—but intellectual honesty and fair representation of the subject matter are expected.

Word count: 2,000–6,000 words (Chinese) or 1,500–4,000 words (English)

CrossOver

Crossover publishes interdisciplinary dialogues, interviews, and co-authored pieces that bring together scholars from different fields with ACGN researchers or practitioners. The goal is to create conversations that neither party could produce alone.

Formats include:
- Structured dialogues between two or more contributors
- Joint essays co-authored by researchers from different disciplines
- Interviews with creators, industry professionals, or scholars
- Roundtable discussions on a shared theme

This section is primarily invitation-based, but unsolicited proposals are welcome. Please contact the editorial office with a brief concept note before full submission.

Word count: 3,000–6,000 words (Chinese) or 2,000–4,000 words (English)

Community

Scope An open forum publishing three distinct types of content. Community content is clearly labeled as such and is editorially distinct from the peer-reviewed sections of the journal.

Community: Announcements

Issued by the ACGN Nexus editorial board. Not open for external submission. 

Word limit: No fixed limit. Peer review: None. Editorial board only.

Community: Correspondence

Short scholarly responses to articles published in ACGN Nexus, or brief position statements on ongoing debates in ACGN studies.

Word limit: 500–1,500 words (excluding references)

Abstract: Not required. Peer review: Editorial review as default. The editor may request external review. 

Community: Omake

Informal, humorous, creative, or celebratory content engaging with ACGN scholarship and culture.

Word limit: None. Abstract: Not required. Peer review: Editorial review as default. The editor may request external review.

AUTHOR RESPONSIBILITY STATEMENT

By submitting a manuscript to ACGN Nexus, all authors confirm and accept the following:

1. Originality: The submitted work is the authors' own. All sources, ideas, and arguments drawn from others are properly cited. Plagiarism in any form is prohibited.

2. No Simultaneous Submission: The manuscript is not currently under consideration by any other journal or publication venue, and will not be submitted elsewhere while under review.

3. No Prior Publication: The work has not been previously published in substantially similar form. Prior posting as a blog post, social media thread, or conference presentation should be disclosed but does not disqualify submission.

4. Authorship: All listed authors have made meaningful intellectual contributions to the work and approve the final manuscript. Ghost authorship and gift authorship are not permitted.

5. Corresponding Author: The corresponding author is authorized by all co-authors to act on their behalf regarding submission, revision, and publication decisions.

6. Accuracy: All author names, affiliations, and contact information are accurate.

7. Conflicts of Interest: All potential conflicts of interest (financial, personal, professional, or ideological) have been disclosed.

8. Copyright Compliance: Any copyrighted material (images, text excerpts, etc.) is used in accordance with fair use principles, with proper attribution.

9. Post-Publication Responsibility: Authors are responsible for the accuracy and integrity of their published work. If significant errors are discovered after publication, authors should notify the editorial office promptly for correction or retraction.

 

PUBLICATION ETHICS

ACGN Nexus is committed to upholding the highest standards of publication ethics. We follow the guidelines established by the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE).


EDITORIAL REVIEW PROCESS

All submissions undergo editorial screening, with optional double-blind peer review when warranted. The process is as follows:

1. Initial Screening — The Editor-in-Chief or Managing Editor checks the submission for scope, completeness, and basic quality. Submissions that do not meet minimum standards may be desk-rejected at this stage.

2. Editorial Assignment — The manuscript is assigned to one or more Editorial Board members with relevant expertise.

3. Editorial Evaluation — Board members evaluate the manuscript for intellectual rigor, originality, clarity, proper citation, and relevance to the journal's scope.

4. Optional Double-Blind Peer Review — When the assigned editors determine that a submission requires domain expertise beyond the Editorial Board's knowledge (e.g., specialized ACGN subcultures, specific social science methodologies, or niche cultural contexts), the manuscript is sent to external reviewers under double-blind conditions: neither the author(s) nor the reviewer(s) know each other's identity.

5. Decision — Based on the editorial evaluation (and external reviews, if conducted), one of four decisions is made:
   - Accept as submitted
   - Accept with minor revisions
   - Revise and resubmit (major revisions required)
   - Decline

6. Author Revision — If revisions are requested, authors are given a reasonable timeframe to respond.

7. Final Decision — The Editor-in-Chief makes the final publication decision.

Typical turnaround: 2–4 weeks for editorial-only review; 4–6 weeks when double-blind peer review is involved.


FOR AUTHORS

- Submit only original work with proper attribution.
- Disclose all conflicts of interest.
- Respond promptly and constructively to editorial feedback.
- Notify the editorial office of any errors discovered after publication.


FOR EDITORS

- Evaluate manuscripts based on intellectual merit, not the author's identity, nationality, gender, or institutional affiliation.
- Maintain confidentiality of submitted manuscripts.
- Recuse themselves from editorial decisions where a conflict of interest exists.
- Make fair, timely, and well-reasoned decisions.


MISCONDUCT

If misconduct is suspected (plagiarism, fabrication, duplicate submission, copyright violation), the editorial office will:
1. Contact the author for explanation.
2. Investigate following COPE guidelines.
3. Take appropriate action, which may include rejection, retraction, or notification of the author's institution.


COMPLAINTS AND APPEALS

Authors who disagree with an editorial decision may submit a written appeal to the Journal Manger at [email protected]. Appeals should include specific reasons why the decision should be reconsidered. The Journal Manger's decision on appeals is final.

Privacy Statement

We process your data based on contractual necessity (to publish your work) and legitimate interest (to maintain the scientific record). Specific uses include:

  • Managing your account, submissions, and editorial workflows.

Personal data associated with published articles (e.g., author names, affiliations) is retained permanently to ensure the integrity of the scientific record and cannot be removed, even upon request. Other personal data (e.g., inactive user accounts) will be retained only as long as necessary for our services.
